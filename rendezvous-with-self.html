Bibhu Acharya · 2010

Rendezvous with Self!

Heart - a hollow muscular organ of vertebrate animals that by its rhythmic contraction acts as a force pump maintaining the circulation of the blood. Seldom has it happened that I take some time off to peep into this muscular organ. It was within one of the early hours of mid-winter when I dared to take a peep into this one again; probably in search of something. Probably wondering how are things going on within this muscular flesh. What changes had occurred inside that has churned my life into normal? Why has the blood pumping speed restored to normal? And if it’s normal, why has there been more rush in my nerves and veins?

With all these thoughts raging a war between my inner self and outer self, I dived into this tunnel; the tunnel that was filled with emotions, feelings, pain, happiness, sorrow, hatred, love the list being endless.

Scared, which I seldom am, I ventured deeper into the dark trenches that lay beneath. I was careful in laying my feet down so as not to bump into any of these entities both from my treacherous past and unseen future. The only courage that propelled me however was the love that I had been craving for since the time immemorial and faith on the Savior Almighty.

Deeper as I crept, I was taken aback by something that lay in front of me. It was glittering from the faint ray of light that somehow managed to find its way through this hole created by this craving. I drew myself nearer. It was some kind of a gift with a marvelous wrapping. The wrapping reminded me of the birthday gifts that were presented by people who loved me ages ago. But was this meant for me? And if it was, who would bless me with such a thing? And why would this person lay it there? Was this person aware of my sudden and unplanned voyage? And if this person was however aware, then this must be someone special who would know the link that holds my inner self and outer self together. The link that has been eluding my comprehension since the time I first saw light on this mundane abode of the Supreme Almighty.

Busy in mind with such questions, I drew myself closer to get a better view of this anonymous gift. But the outburst of anxiety was jolted with a voice that seemed to emanate from the wrapped box.” Come here you, closer, closer! Take a look. Tear me apart and you will find your Pandora’s Box awaiting you desperately.” My greedy mind was lured by the voice. Millions of neurons signaled a lot of activity in the dense grey matter within billionth of a second. Such bliss of being bestowed on with the Pandora’s Box dragged my shadow deeper into the dungeon where lay dormant the monsters such as greed, lust, desire, expectations.

I stretched out my desperate hands to untie the beautiful knot that kept the Pandora’s Box just wrapper distance away from me. As the knot slipped through its own silky friction, my hungry eyes caught a glimpse of the inner beauty; the marvel. I was mutely lost in the chaos of my own vocabulary.

Just a glimpse revealed such pious and divinity. Unraveling it would insult its sanctity. Such whiteness that sparkles purity Debar me O Deity From spoiling this chastity.

Such splendid was the sight that made my eyes roll down with respect, shyness and guilt of defaming the holiness. How on earth could I? Thank you Lord for preventing me from such an offence! Thank you O my Love for being besides me to make me realize my crime. With my eyes still down I wrapped back the cover, left the gift as The Supreme Being wanted it to be left back and turned around for my voyage back. Somewhere from the depth of the tunnel came an echo,

Duty Done Wrapper will be undone Cultivate faith Pandora will be your wealth Success will be laid down By Time’s Bright Long Gown!

With a satisfied grin I strolled back the Treacherous Tunnel. Today I was promoted to an Explorer.
