Meet the Founder

Bibhu “Bibs” Acharya

An engineer by profession, a writer by passion, and an explorer by nature.

Some people spend their lives searching for answers. Others discover that the beauty of life lies in never running out of questions.

Bibhu “Bibs” Acharya has always belonged a little to both worlds.

Born in Cuttack, Odisha, India, and raised in the small town of Keonjhar, Bibs grew up with a curiosity that would eventually carry him far beyond the familiar boundaries of home. His journey has taken him across oceans, cultures, professions, and continents. Yet through it all, one fascination has remained constant: the extraordinary possibilities hidden within the human mind and spirit.

His professional journey began with the Indian Navy, where he discovered the value of discipline, responsibility, courage, and service. What followed was an international career spanning more than two decades in naval architecture, shipbuilding, maritime engineering, and project management.

From the shipyards of South Korea, China, and Japan to professional assignments across Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and the United States, his work has brought him into contact with people from remarkably different backgrounds. These experiences taught him something that no engineering textbook could fully explain: although our languages, traditions, and circumstances may differ, our deepest human aspirations are often remarkably similar.

Academically, Bibs holds a bachelor's degree in Naval Architecture and Shipbuilding from Cochin University of Science and Technology, India; a master's degree in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering from Chosun University, South Korea; and a second master's degree in Management Information Systems from California, United States.

Engineering trained him to understand how things work. Life inspired him to ask why they matter.

Beyond the technical world, Bibs is a poet, author, motivational speaker, and lifelong student of human potential. A voracious reader, he has been deeply influenced by literature exploring the relationship between thought, belief, purpose, prosperity, and personal transformation.

Books such as The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho, Think and Grow Rich by Napoleon Hill, The Secret by Rhonda Byrne, The Power of Your Subconscious Mind by Joseph Murphy, and As a Man Thinketh by James Allen have helped shape his intellectual curiosity. He is equally drawn to the insights of psychology, philosophy, spirituality, and the behavioural sciences.

He does not regard any single book, teacher, or philosophy as possessing every answer. For him, wisdom is something to be explored, questioned, experienced, and shared.

A lover of languages, Bibs speaks Odia, Hindi, English, and Korean, along with conversational Chinese for everyday situations. He is always eager to learn another language—not simply to communicate, but to discover another way of understanding the world.

He is also an enthusiastic traveller, an adventurous spirit, and an unapologetic lover of good food. Whether discovering an unfamiliar cuisine, exploring a new city, attempting an adventure sport, or sharing a conversation with someone from a different culture, he finds joy in experiences that broaden his understanding of life.

His spiritual outlook is deeply personal and continually evolving. Although he respects the wisdom and traditions of all religions, he does not define himself through allegiance to any particular one.

For Bibs, humanity is the highest expression of religion.

He believes that kindness requires no common language, compassion needs no religious affiliation, and helping another human being is among the most meaningful things a person can do.

This belief lies at the heart of his writing.

His book, 1600 Mega Joules: Awaken the Hidden Sun Within, explores the extraordinary potential that often remains dormant beneath fear, habit, uncertainty, and self-imposed limitations. Drawing upon philosophy, psychology, spirituality, and lived experience, it invites readers to reconsider what they believe is possible.

Yet Bibs does not present himself as someone who has mastered life or discovered its ultimate secrets.

He considers himself a student—sometimes confident, sometimes uncertain, occasionally mistaken, but always willing to learn.

For him, the pursuit of wisdom is not about becoming superior to others. It is about becoming more conscious of oneself, more compassionate toward others, and more useful to the world.

That philosophy inspired the creation of Āchārya Prapañca, an evolving home for literature, ideas, heritage, learning, and future creative endeavours.

Its purpose is both ambitious and profoundly simple: to share knowledge that encourages thought, inspires personal awakening, and contributes, however modestly, to a more conscious and compassionate humanity.

Bibs believes that meaningful change does not always begin with grand movements or extraordinary individuals.

Sometimes, it begins with a book.

Sometimes, with a conversation.

Sometimes, with one person choosing to think differently.

And sometimes, that one person changes everything.
